What This Study Is About

We want to hear YOUR opinions.

  • New Heart Treatment: Scientists are working on a new therapy using special cells that could help hearts work better
  • Your Voice Matters: Before testing begins, we need to know what heart failure patients think about this new approach
  • Just Talking: You would share your thoughts in a 1-hour conversation - no medical procedures involved
  • Help Shape the Future: Your opinions will help researchers design better studies and treatments

Think of this as a chance to have your voice heard in the future of heart failure care!

Who We Are

This is a collaborative project led by researchers at Macquarie University, The Westmead Institute of Medical Research, The George Institute for Global Health, and Sydney Health Ethics.

What's Involved for Participants

  • Participation involves a single 60-75 minute interview conducted over Zoom or telephone
  • Interviews are audio-recorded with permission
  • Participants will receive a $50 electronic gift voucher to thank them for their time

Who Can Join This Study?

We're looking for people who are:

  • 18 years old or older
  • Living with heart failure
  • Comfortable speaking English
  • Willing to share their experiences and thoughts
